# Join the Unstuck beta

A short, calm walkthrough — about two minutes, no rush. You can stop anywhere and come back.

Unstuck isn't on the App Store yet. While it's in **beta**, you try it through **TestFlight** — Apple's free, official app for testing apps early. Here's the whole path.

---

## What you'll need

- An **iPhone** (or iPad) on **iOS 18 or newer**.
- About **two minutes**.
- That's it — no account, no sign-up, nothing to pay.

---

## 1. Get TestFlight (one time)

TestFlight is made by Apple. It's how you install apps that aren't public yet.

**[Install TestFlight from the App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664)**

If you already have it, skip ahead.

---

## 2. Open the beta invite

You'll get a **beta link**. Tap it **on your iPhone** and it opens straight in TestFlight.

> **The public beta link will live right here once the beta opens.**
> Until then, email **[deniztimbiloglu@gmail.com](mailto:deniztimbiloglu@gmail.com)** and you'll get an invite.

In TestFlight, tap **Accept**, then **Install**.

*Got a redeem code instead of a link?* Open TestFlight ▸ **Redeem** ▸ type the code.

---

## 3. Install and open

Unstuck lands on your home screen like any app — a small **orange dot** just marks it as a beta.

Open it. There's a calm three-breath intro you can skip. It only asks for a permission **the moment it needs one**, and you can say **no** to any of them — it still works either way. Nothing it reads ever leaves your phone.

---

## 4. Try one thing

Type or say a thought — anything in your head right now. Watch it find its spot on the map. That's the whole loop, and it's the fastest way to feel what this is.

*Optional:* turn your **Action Button** into instant voice capture, for when stopping to type would lose the thought —
**Settings ▸ Action Button ▸ Shortcut ▸ Unstuck ▸ "Capture a Thought."**

---

## Your mind stays yours

Everything lives **on your device**. No account, nothing collected, nothing sent anywhere — there's nothing to sign up for and no one watching. ([Full privacy policy →](privacy.html))

---

- Or email **[deniztimbiloglu@gmail.com](mailto:deniztimbiloglu@gmail.com)**.

## Good to know

- **New versions arrive on their own** through TestFlight — no need to check.
- A beta build lasts **90 days**, then TestFlight gently points you to the next one.
- Use it once a day or once a month. **Coming and going is normal here**, never a fail — the app is built for exactly that.
