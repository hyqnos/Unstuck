# Privacy Policy — Unstuck

**Effective date:** June 2026

Unstuck is built around one simple promise: **your mind stays yours.**

## The short version

**Unstuck does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data.** There are no accounts, no sign-up, no email, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything you put into the app stays **on your device**.

## What the app can access (and what happens to it)

With your permission, Unstuck may read the following — **always locally, always read-only, and none of it ever leaves your device:**

- **Calendar & Reminders** — to show your upcoming events and due reminders on your map, and to gently reschedule an overlapping item into a free slot. When you choose to move an event or reminder, that change is written back through Apple's system frameworks to **your own** calendar/reminder accounts (Apple, Google, Outlook, etc.). The developer never sees it.
- **Health data** (steps, sleep, heart rate, active energy) — to show your body's patterns on your map. Read-only; never stored off-device.
- **Microphone & Speech recognition** — only while *you* start a voice capture. The transcription becomes a note on your map. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

You can decline any of these and the app still works. You can revoke any permission at any time in **iOS Settings**.

## Children & students

Unstuck collects **no** personal information from **anyone**, including children and students. Because nothing is gathered, transmitted, or linked to an identity, there is no personal data to protect, sell, or expose. The app contains no ads and no third-party SDKs.

## Your control

- All app data lives on your device. **Deleting the app removes it.**
- No data is backed up to the developer or any server.
- Turn off any system permission whenever you like.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
